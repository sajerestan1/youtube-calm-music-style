## Album concept

Working name: The Long Way Home
Listener use: Gentle, warm songs for the ride home or the first quiet hour after work.
Sound: Calm modern folk-pop with soft male vocals, felt piano, clean fingerpicked guitar, rounded bass, light sustained strings, and very restrained percussion. Keep the pulse gentle and the vocal close and natural. Avoid heavy drums, dramatic builds, belting, and busy arrangements.
Target: At least 1 hour 30 minutes in the final verified export. These 12 originals are a track concept list; add or repeat only approved music until the measured album reaches the target.
